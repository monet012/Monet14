Gist: how to raise your GPA

  • Chase high-unit courses first. A 4-unit course moves your GPA twice as much as a 2-unit one.
  • Attend every class and practical. CA (continuous assessment) usually carries 30–40% of the score, so it is easy marks.
  • Never skip CA tests and assignments. Zero in CA is hard to recover from in the exam.
  • Use past questions from your department and study in a group for hard courses.
  • Resit failed courses early. An F adds units but zero points; clearing it lifts your CGPA.
  • Plan your reading two weeks before exams, not the night before.
  • Talk to your course adviser early if you are below 2.00 (risk of probation).

Use the "Biggest drag" list on the GPA tab to see which courses cost you the most points.
